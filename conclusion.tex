\chapter{Conclusion \& Future Scope}

\section{Conclusion}
This Phase 2 project developed a complete automatic modulation classification
pipeline from raw I/Q samples to quantitative evaluation. A compact
one-dimensional CNN was trained on RadioML 2016.10a using normalized samples,
stratified data splits, class-weighted cross-entropy, AdamW optimization, and
cosine learning-rate scheduling. The model achieved 53.94\% held-out test
accuracy, while SNR-wise accuracy increased from 9.24\% at -20 dB to 81.68\%
at +12 dB. The result demonstrates that deep learning can learn useful
modulation-discriminative representations directly from baseband data.

\section{Future Scope}
Future work will include signal augmentation, residual and attention-based
architectures, calibration and confidence estimation, cross-dataset
validation, and over-the-air testing with an SDR platform. A larger training
budget and systematic hyperparameter study can further improve the benchmark
score and robustness to frequency offset, timing offset, fading, and
transmitter hardware imperfections.
